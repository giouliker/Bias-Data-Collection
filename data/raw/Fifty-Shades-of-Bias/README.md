# Fifty Shades of Bias (FSB)

### Research Focus
This dataset focuses on gender bias within English text generated by Large Language Models (GPT). Unlike binary classification, it uses a nuanced approach to measure the *degree* of bias, capturing subtle stereotypes and social prejudices that are often missed by traditional "offensive" filters.

### Dataset Characteristics
* **Total Rows:** ~10,000+ sentences with aggregate scores / ~50,000+ individual annotations.
* **Data Origins:** GPT-generated text rated by human annotators.
* **Research Source:** Microsoft Research (2022).
* **License:** MIT
* **URL:** [GitHub Link](https://github.com/microsoft/fifty-shades-of-bias)

### Human Annotations
* **Methodology:** Best-Worst Scaling (BWS). Annotators compared sets of sentences to determine which was more or less biased, rather than providing absolute ratings.
* **Nuance:** This approach moves beyond binary labels (bias vs. no-bias) to provide a continuous scale of gender-based stereotypes.
